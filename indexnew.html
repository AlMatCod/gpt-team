About GPTteam

A student-led robotics team #34155 from Karaganda. We build robots, develop technologies, and create opportunities for the STEM community of Kazakhstan.

Who We Are

🤖 Robotics Team

GPTteam is a team of students from Karaganda region united by a passion for robotics and innovation. We participate in international FIRST competitions and build robots that solve real-world challenges.

🎓 Educational Mission

Our goal is not only to win, but to develop STEM education in Kazakhstan, support young teams, and create a community of roboticists.

⚡ Innovation & Teamwork

Every team member brings unique contributions: from programming and engineering design to strategy and project management. Together, we create something greater than just a robot.
